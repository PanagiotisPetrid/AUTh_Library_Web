Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

Section 01

Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

No Personal DataWe do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information.
No Background TrackingThe App does not track your behavior, usage patterns, or location in the background.
Local Data OnlyThe App uses a small local file (SharedPreferences) on your device to remember if you dismissed the "How to Use" instructions. This data never leaves your device.

Section 02

Permissions

No Internet AccessThe App does not request or use the Android Internet permission. It is physically incapable of sending data from your device to an external server.
No Location AccessThe App does not use GPS or network-based location services.

Section 03

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or AdMob).

Section 04

User-Initiated Sharing

The App allows you to manually share a link of a table location. This action is entirely controlled by you.

When you choose to share, the App passes the information to the specific third-party application you select (e.g., Viber, Instagram, or System Clipboard). We do not have access to the data once it is passed to those apps.

Section 05

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
