Privacy Policy for Find Me (AUTh Central Library) Effective Date: May 15, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the Find Me mobile application (the "App") treats user data. We built this app to be a privacy-first utility for students at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. 1. Information Collection and Use The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information. No Background Tracking: The App does not track your behavior, usage patterns, or location in the background. Local Data Only: The App uses a small local file (SharedPreferences) on your device to remember if you have dismissed the "How to Use" instructions. This data never leaves your device. 2. Permissions No Internet Access: The App does not request or use the Android Internet permission. It is physically incapable of sending data from your device to an external server. No Location Access: The App does not use GPS or network-based location services. 3. Third-Party Services The App does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising networks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or AdMob). 4. User-Initiated Sharing The App allows you to manually share a link or an image of a table location. This action is entirely controlled by you. When you choose to share, the App passes the information to the specific third-party application you select (e.g., Viber, Instagram, or System Clipboard). We do not have access to the data once it is passed to those apps. 5. Children’s Privacy The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. 6.
