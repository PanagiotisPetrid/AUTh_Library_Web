Troubleshooting

Link didn't open the app?

Follow the steps for your device to enable automatic deep links.

Quick workaround while you fix it

Open the Find Me app, tap the Search button, and type the table name from the link (e.g. A4). It will highlight the exact table right away.

Samsung (One UI)

Samsung devices

Samsung's One UI sometimes disables app link verification by default.

1
Open Settings Go to your phone's main Settings app.
2
Find the App Tap Apps, then find and tap Find Me in the list.
3
Open "Set as default" Scroll down and tap Set as default. Settings → Apps → Find Me → Set as default
4
Tap "Supported web addresses" You'll see a list of web addresses the app can handle.
5
Enable the domain Make sure panagiotispetrid.github.io is toggled on. If it's off, tap it to enable.
6
Try the link again Ask your friend to re-send you the link, or tap it again from your messages.
Pixel / Stock Android

Google Pixel & stock Android

On standard Android, you can manage which links open in the app directly from App Info.

1
Open Settings → Apps Find and tap Find Me.
2
Tap "Open by default" Settings → Apps → Find Me → Open by default
3
Tap "Add link" A list of supported web addresses appears.
4
Check panagiotispetrid.github.io Make sure the domain is checked, then tap Add.
Xiaomi / MIUI / HyperOS

Xiaomi devices

MIUI and HyperOS have an extra security layer that can block link interception.

1
Open Settings → Apps Tap Manage apps, then find Find Me.
2
Tap "Open by default" or "Default apps" The label varies by MIUI version. Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Find Me → Open by default
3
Enable "Open supported links" Toggle this on if it appears. Then verify that panagiotispetrid.github.io is listed and enabled.

MIUI tip: If the option is greyed out, go to Settings → Additional settings → Privacy → Special app access → Opening links and enable it for Find Me there instead.

Huawei / EMUI / HarmonyOS

Huawei devices

1
Open Settings → Apps → Apps Find Find Me and tap it.
2
Tap "Default app settings" or "Open by default" Settings → Apps → Find Me → Default app settings
3
Enable the supported domain Toggle on panagiotispetrid.github.io.
All devices

Still not working? Try these

Clear app defaults. In Settings → Apps → Find Me, look for Clear defaults or Open by default → Clear defaults. Then tap the shared link again and choose Find Me from the picker — select "Always".

Reinstall the app. Uninstall Find Me, then reinstall it from the Google Play Store. Android re-runs link verification on a fresh install, which often resolves the issue automatically.

Check your browser. If you're tapping the link inside an app like Instagram or Gmail, those apps use a built-in browser that may not hand off to Find Me. Try copying the link and opening it in Chrome instead.

Restart your phone. After changing any of the settings above, a restart ensures Android applies the new defaults cleanly.
