Samsung (One UI)

One UI sometimes disables link verification by default. Enable it manually below.

1
Open Settings Go to your phone's main Settings app.
2
Go to Apps Tap Apps, then find and tap Find Me in the list.
3
Tap "Set as default" Scroll down inside the app's settings page. Settings → Apps → Find Me → Set as default
4
Tap "Supported web addresses" A list of web addresses the app can handle will appear.
5
Enable the domain Make sure panagiotispetrid.github.io is toggled on. If it's off, tap it to enable it.
6
Try the link again Ask your friend to re-send the link, or tap it again from your messages.

Google Pixel & Stock Android

On standard Android you manage which links open in the app directly from App Info.

1
Open Settings → Apps Find and tap Find Me in the app list.
2
Tap "Open by default" Settings → Apps → Find Me → Open by default
3
Tap "Add link" A list of supported web addresses will appear.
4
Check the domain Make sure panagiotispetrid.github.io is checked, then tap Add.
5
Try the link again Tap the shared link from your messages app.

Xiaomi (MIUI / HyperOS)

MIUI and HyperOS have an extra security layer that can block link interception.

1
Open Settings → Apps → Manage apps Find and tap Find Me.
2
Tap "Open by default" The label may say Default apps depending on your MIUI version. Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Find Me → Open by default
3
Enable "Open supported links" Toggle this on. Then confirm that panagiotispetrid.github.io is listed and enabled.
4
Try the link again Tap the shared link from your messages app.

Still greyed out? Go to Settings → Additional settings → Privacy → Special app access → Opening links and enable it for Find Me there instead.

Huawei (EMUI / HarmonyOS)

Huawei devices handle default app links through App Info settings.

1
Open Settings → Apps → Apps Find and tap Find Me.
2
Tap "Default app settings" May also appear as Open by default. Settings → Apps → Find Me → Default app settings
3
Enable the supported domain Toggle on panagiotispetrid.github.io.
4
Try the link again Tap the shared link from your messages app.

OPPO & OnePlus (ColorOS / OxygenOS)

ColorOS and OxygenOS follow the standard Android path but may label things slightly differently.

1
Open Settings → Apps → App Management Find and tap Find Me.
2
Tap "Default app settings" or "Open by default" Settings → Apps → Find Me → Default app settings
3
Enable "Open supported links" Make sure panagiotispetrid.github.io is listed and enabled.
4
Try the link again Tap the shared link from your messages app.

OnePlus tip: On newer OxygenOS 14+, the path may be Settings → Apps → See all apps → Find Me → Open by default.

Other Android devices

Most Android phones follow a similar path, even if the labels differ slightly.

1
Open Settings → Apps Find and tap Find Me in the app list.
2
Look for "Open by default" or "Set as default" This is usually near the bottom of the app's info page.
3
Enable the domain Look for panagiotispetrid.github.io and make sure it is toggled on or checked.
4
Try the link again Tap the shared link from your messages app.
Last resort

Still not working? Try these.

Clear app defaults In Settings → Apps → Find Me, tap Clear defaults or Open by default → Clear defaults. Then tap the link again and choose Find Me → "Always".
Reinstall the app Uninstall Find Me, then reinstall it from the Google Play Store. Android re-runs link verification on a fresh install, which often resolves the issue automatically.
Open from Chrome instead Apps like Instagram and Gmail use a built-in browser that may not hand off links to Find Me. Copy the link and open it in Chrome — it should trigger the app correctly.
Restart your phone After changing any of the settings above, a restart ensures Android applies the new defaults cleanly.
