Why join

Why become a tester?

Google Play requires at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 consecutive days before an app can be published publicly. As an early tester you get access before anyone else, and any bugs you find or suggestions you make will be directly addressed.

There's no commitment beyond installing the app and trying it out. You can leave the testing programme at any time.

Your privacy is fully protected. Find Me collects zero personal data — no accounts, no analytics, no location tracking. Your only role as a tester is to use it and let us know what you think.
